# Emily Miaw — Channel Brief

**Status:** Creative development / pre-production  
**First project:** Kawaii Kids Storytelling #001  
**Format target:** YouTube Shorts, 45–60 seconds  
**Storytelling mode:** Silent visual storytelling; no spoken narration  
**Visual medium:** Handmade miniature stop-motion aesthetic, combining wool felt and matte polymer clay  
**Audio direction:** Playful plucks, gentle musical motifs, expressive comedy sound effects  
**Approval policy:** No asset generation, production jobs, publishing, or production-file changes without explicit approval.

## Channel identity

Emily Miaw is a warm, playful, character-led world for young children. Stories should be understandable from actions, expressions, staging, and sound alone, even when watched without dialogue. Favor kindness, curiosity, small surprises, gentle comedy, and emotionally reassuring endings.

Create original characters and original worlds. Do not imitate protected character designs, logos, or recognizable franchise-specific details.

## Audience and editorial principles

- Keep conflicts gentle and emotionally safe; resolve them through curiosity, cooperation, sharing, or care.
- Use clear visual cause and effect and one primary action per shot.
- Avoid frightening imagery, peril that lingers, humiliating jokes, unsafe imitation, and manipulative engagement tactics.
- Do not rely on readable generated text in the image.
- Design for mobile 9:16 framing with clear silhouettes and readable expressions.
- Keep stories accessible without narration or subtitles.

## Production workflow

1. Revise and approve the story premise.
2. Approve character identities and the visual bible.
3. Create character sheets and visual references only after explicit approval.
4. Draft a shot-by-shot storyboard and silent-audio plan.
5. Review storyboard and prompts before generating stills.
6. Generate stills, then perform continuity and quality checks.
7. Animate approved stills, assemble, and inspect the final video and audio.
8. Publish only after explicit final approval.
