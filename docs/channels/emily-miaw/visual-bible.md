# Emily Miaw — Visual Bible v0.1

**Status:** Draft for review; not an approved production spec.

## Core visual idea

A tactile handmade miniature world photographed like a stop-motion set. Combine soft wool felt characters with matte polymer-clay details and small handcrafted props. The result should feel cozy, imperfect in a charming way, and physically touchable—not glossy, generic CGI.

## Material language

- **Characters:** visible fine wool fibers, soft felt surfaces, subtle stitched seams where appropriate.
- **Small details:** matte polymer clay for noses, clips, buttons, and tiny accessories.
- **Props and sets:** miniature craft materials, rounded edges, handmade details, believable small-scale construction.
- Avoid shiny plastic, wet-looking surfaces, hyperreal skin/fur, excessive texture noise, and polished toy-commercial CGI.

## Draft palette

| Name | Hex | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| Warm Ivory | `#F2E7D5` | Neutral backgrounds, cream felt |
| Soft Lilac | `#C7B4E5` | Pip's main color and accents |
| Gentle Mint | `#B9DCC5` | Mimi's accessory and calming set accents |
| Butter Yellow | `#F4D27F` | Small props and cheerful highlights |
| Soft Blush | `#E8A9A4` | Cheeks and restrained warm accents |

Keep contrast sufficient to separate characters from the set. Treat these values as a starting point, not locked production color data.

## Lighting and lens language

- Soft studio key light with gentle fill and a subtle rim to separate felt silhouettes.
- Warm, inviting white balance; preserve the natural material colors.
- Macro miniature-photography feeling with shallow depth of field, but keep eyes and the main action in focus.
- Avoid extreme bokeh that hides story-critical props or facial expressions.
- Keep lighting direction and intensity consistent within a scene and across connected shots.

## Composition

- Default deliverable framing: vertical 9:16 for Shorts.
- Favor simple, uncluttered backgrounds and clear foreground/midground separation.
- Keep eyes, gestures, and story-critical props inside a central mobile-safe area.
- Use a stable miniature-set geography; screen direction and prop positions must remain consistent between shots.
- One primary action per shot. Each frame should communicate a clear beat without text.

## Motion language

Use small, deliberate stop-motion-inspired movements:
- short hops, ear flops, tail reactions, head tilts, eye-line shifts, paw gestures;
- tiny prop bounces, rolls, wiggles, and carefully staged reveals;
- gentle camera slides, pans, or short push-ins when they serve the story.

Avoid continuous slow push-ins as the only movement, frantic camera motion, complex finger/face articulation, elastic anatomy, uncontrolled morphing, or multiple competing actions in one shot. Motion should feel handcrafted rather than mechanically smooth.

## Continuity locks

Before image generation, define and approve:
1. Master front, three-quarter, side, and back views for each character.
2. Fixed body proportions, facial layout, colors, material, and accessories.
3. A scene/set reference with stable object positions and lighting direction.
4. A prop list with color, scale, and initial/final positions.
5. A shot continuity checklist for screen direction, eyelines, scale, and entrances/exits.

Never use a different character design to patch a continuity error without approval.

## Prompt foundation (draft only)

> Create an original handmade miniature stop-motion character. Tactile wool felt and matte polymer clay, visible fine fibers, handcrafted seams, rounded silhouette, expressive simple eyes, soft studio lighting, real miniature-set photography. Show front, three-quarter, side, and back views of the same character. Maintain identical proportions, material, colors, and accessories. Neutral background, consistent scale, no text, no logos.

This is a prompt draft only. It has not been sent to an image generator.

## Negative direction

No franchise character lookalikes; no logos or watermarks; no readable AI-generated text; no extra limbs or duplicated accessories; no inconsistent eye placement; no glossy plastic; no photorealistic human features; no dramatic horror lighting; no uncontrolled changes in costume or scale.
