# Roblox Video Format System — Keep the channel fresh without losing identity

## Content principle

Do not make every upload a ten-item myth list. Keep the **same premium investigator avatar and visual brand**, but rotate the viewer promise, pacing, game, challenge and emotional payoff. No format guarantees retention; judge it using YouTube Studio impressions, CTR, first-30-second retention, average view duration, and returning viewers.

## Repeatable formats

| Format | Core promise | Suggested length | Hook pattern |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Myth Lab | Test claims with evidence | 8–11 min | “Everyone says X. We tested it.” |
| Secret / Easter Egg Hunt | Find hidden rooms, codes, endings | 6–10 min | Show a strange clue, withhold answer briefly |
| One-Life Challenge | Complete a task with a strict rule | 8–12 min | State the fail condition immediately |
| Horror Game Guide | Safe routes, puzzle solutions, endings | 6–10 min | Show the obstacle and promise a practical solution |
| Scary Story Investigation | Investigate a legend inside a game | 7–12 min | Start with the most intriguing verified clue |
| Update Breakdown | Explain new monsters, maps or mechanics | 4–8 min | Show what changed, then explain why it matters |
| Tier List | Rank monsters, maps, items or scares | 7–10 min | Open with a controversial but defensible placement |
| Old vs New | Compare versions, maps, mechanics or graphics | 6–9 min | Fast A/B visual comparison |
| Viewer Challenge | Attempt a viewer-submitted rule | 7–11 min | Read the challenge and its fail condition |
| Speedrun / Route Test | Find the safest or fastest route | 6–10 min | Show target time/route, then test it |
| Randomizer / Wheel | Randomly choose rules, tools or locations | 8–12 min | Reveal the random rule and consequences |
| Co-op / Solo Comparison | Test whether solo or team play changes difficulty | 8–12 min | Present one measurable question |
| No-HUD / Limited Tools | Complete a task with reduced information | 6–10 min | Show what is removed and why it matters |
| “Can You Survive?” | Structured survival challenge | 8–12 min | State timer, objective and fail condition |
| Short Mystery | One question, one result | 20–45 sec | Immediate visual question, fast evidence, result |
| Shorts Highlight | Best moment from long-form | 15–35 sec | Start at the moment of tension, not a channel intro |

## Retention structure for long-form

- **0–5s:** show a real high-stakes moment or question; no greeting.
- **5–20s:** explain the promise and the rules.
- **Every 20–40s:** deliver a meaningful change: new evidence, location, obstacle, result, visual reveal or decision. Do not add empty cuts just to look fast.
- **Every 60–90s:** introduce a larger question, complication, or progress update.
- **Midpoint:** summarize progress and change the stakes.
- **Final 15–25%:** deliver the promised result, explain limitations, and tease a genuinely related next episode.
- Remove repetitive greetings, long loading screens, redundant explanations, and filler CTA breaks.

## Accuracy and trust rules

1. Claims are hypotheses until supported by captured footage.
2. Identify the Roblox experience and relevant version/update when mechanics may vary.
3. Keep uncut evidence available for review; label recreations and illustrative AI images.
4. Use CONFIRMED only when the evidence supports the stated claim; BUSTED only when the test meaningfully contradicts it; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE.
5. Never invent views, player counts, update details, game mechanics, results or “secret” discoveries.
6. If a test fails, that can be the story. Show what happened honestly.
7. Do not promise that every viewer will watch to the end. Run small experiments and learn from analytics.

## Thumbnail system

- One dominant character face/pose, one threat/object, one clear emotional question.
- At most 2–4 short words in post-production; ensure readability on mobile.
- Make the title and thumbnail complementary, not duplicates.
- Avoid fake arrows, fake UI, false claims, or a “secret found” promise without proof.
- Produce 2–3 variants and compare CTR alongside retention, not CTR alone.

## Sustainable upload mix

For every 4 long-form uploads, test a balanced mix such as:
- 1 Myth Lab
- 1 Secret Hunt or Guide
- 1 Challenge / Viewer Challenge
- 1 Tier List, Update Breakdown, or Old vs New

Cut 2–3 Shorts from genuinely compelling moments in each long-form video, but ensure each Short has a complete mini-story. Adjust the mix from audience analytics rather than assuming one format always wins.
